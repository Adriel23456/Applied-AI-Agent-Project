# What This Contains — `03Evidence`

The trail of the team's progress. Not the formal deliverables — the small wins along the way.

## Contents

- Meeting notes: what we discussed, what we decided, who took what.
- Agreements reached by the team, and the reasoning behind them.
- Milestones and landmarks: the moment something first worked, first compiled, first trained, first got approved.
- Screenshots, exports and short logs that back any of the above.

This is not the place for the professor's official approvals, the final figures of the paper, or the graded artifacts — those live where the deliverable lives.

## Why we keep it

This folder is the raw material for the **work journal** required in the next stage of the project. Writing that journal from memory at the end of the semester is how teams lose points; writing it from here takes an afternoon.

## Rules

- Add entries as they happen, not in batches at the end of a sprint.
- Date every entry and name who was there.
- Short is fine. Three lines beats nothing.
