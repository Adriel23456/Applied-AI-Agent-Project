# What This Contains — `03Evidence`

The trail of the team's progress. Not the formal deliverables — the small wins along the way.

## Contents

- **`00WorkJournalLatex/`**: The LaTeX source files for compiling the formal work journal.
- **`01AIUsage/`**: The directory dedicated to our AI tool usage declarations. Each stage gets its own markdown file here (e.g., `AIUsageStage1.md`) to maintain a clear, cumulative record of our AI assistance across the semester.
- **Root-level evidence**: Screenshots, exports, meeting notes, and short logs that back our progress, milestones, and team agreements (e.g., `21-8-26_TeamFirstMeeting.png`, `26-8-26_ThemeConfirmation.png`). These capture the moment something first worked, first compiled, or was decided.

*Note: This is not the place for the professor's official approvals, the final figures of the paper, or the graded artifacts — those live where the deliverable lives.*

## Why we keep it

This folder is the raw material for the **work journal** required in the next stage of the project. Writing that journal from memory at the end of the semester is how teams lose points; compiling it from this evidence folder takes an afternoon.

## Rules

- Add entries and evidence as they happen, not in batches at the end of a sprint.
- Date every file and note who was involved.
- Short is fine. Three lines of reasoning or a single screenshot beats nothing.
