\section{Purpose and Conventions}
\label{sec:conventions}

\subsection{Purpose}
This journal records the development of the project across its three
stages: what was decided, why, what blocked progress, and what evidence
backs each claim. It is not the paper and it is not the sprint plan. The
paper reports results; the sprint plan states intent; this document
records what actually happened.

\subsection{How Entries Are Written}
Every entry opens with a dated header naming the owner and the branch the
work happened on. Inside an entry, three callouts carry the weight:

\decision{The choice that was made.}{Why it was made, with evidence.}{The
alternatives considered and the reason each was dropped.}

\blocker{What stopped progress.}{What it delayed and by how much.}{How it
was resolved, or the condition that will resolve it.}

\milestone{What was achieved.}{Where the proof lives: commit, log,
figure, screenshot.}

\notebox[Writing rule]{Write entries the day the work happens. An entry
written a week later loses the reasoning, which is the only part of this
document that cannot be reconstructed from the repository history.}

\subsection{Traceability}
Every decision recorded here that affects a technical choice must appear,
in condensed form, in the corresponding section of the paper. Every
milestone must point at a committed artifact. AI tool usage is annotated
inline and consolidated in \code{AI\_USAGE.md}.
